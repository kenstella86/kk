About Us

Local Expertise, Compassionate Support

Kenstella Care is a China-based medical escort and healthcare concierge service. We support international patients and their families who are considering traveling to China for medical treatment, a second opinion, a health checkup, or surgery.

Navigating a foreign healthcare system can be overwhelming — language barriers, unfamiliar hospital processes, appointments, transportation, and accommodation. Our team handles the coordination so you can focus on what matters most: your health.

  • Local team based in China with on-the-ground hospital experience
  • Access to a wide network of top-tier hospitals across major Chinese cities
  • Clear communication in English throughout your entire journey
  • Transparent service fees with no hidden charges
Our Services

Complete Medical Travel Support

From the first message to your follow-up visit, we coordinate every non-medical detail of your trip to China.

Appointment Booking

We assist with booking appointments at top-tier Grade 3A public hospitals across China, based on your condition and preferred city.

Specialist Recommendation

Based on your medical reports and goals, we help identify suitable hospitals, departments, and specialists for your case.

Medical Record Translation

We help organize and translate your medical records into Chinese so hospital teams can review your case efficiently.

Medical Interpretation

English-speaking medical interpreters accompany you to consultations, examinations, and procedures.

Hospital Navigation & Escort

We guide you through registration, payment, examinations, and pharmacy pick-up — so you never get lost in a large hospital.

Accommodation Arrangement

We recommend and book hotels or serviced apartments near your hospital, matched to your budget and length of stay.

Airport Pickup & Transportation

Airport transfers, hospital shuttle, and daily transport arranged throughout your stay in China.

Visa Invitation Letter Assistance

We help coordinate the documentation you may need when applying for a medical visa to China.

Follow-up & Aftercare

After your visit, we help coordinate follow-up appointments, report delivery, and ongoing communication with the hospital.
